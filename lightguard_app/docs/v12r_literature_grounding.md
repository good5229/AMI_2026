# v0.12R Literature Grounding

Twenty-one unique primary or authoritative sources were reconciled under a search protocol frozen before screening. Nineteen are quality A and two are quality B.

The reviewed research supports four bounded mechanisms:

- Expected lighting state versus electrical/optical observation mismatch can be an inspection signal.
- Sustained meter-relative load departure can be a persistence anomaly sign when the baseline is causal and stable.
- Phase-current asymmetry can be a diagnostic observation, but RMS magnitudes alone are not negative-sequence current.
- Historical baselines are counterfactual references, not outcome labels.

The starting ECCE study validates a laboratory LDR-cover intervention rather than field failure. Road-lighting AMI studies screen cabinet-level departures but do not identify a physical cause without topology and outcomes. Theft and benchmark studies provide methodology and leakage controls, not streetlight fault labels.

Evidence grades are explanatory support, never fault probability. Literature remains independent from H1 and proxy scores and cannot replace the missing Gold/Silver join.
