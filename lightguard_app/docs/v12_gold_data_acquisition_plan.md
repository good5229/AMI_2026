# v0.12 Gold and Silver Data Acquisition Plan

## Priority data

1. Versioned AMI meter to cabinet, circuit, phase, and fixture mapping.
2. Controller/photocell expected state, command, acknowledgement, override, and timestamp.
3. Maintenance/work-order outcome with inspection time, finding, action, and cause.
4. Complaint/dispatch/inspection disposition with explicit unknown and negative definitions.
5. Synchronized voltage, current, power, channel-quality, clock, and meter-calibration provenance.

## Prospective protocol

- Freeze candidate rules before outcomes.
- Blind inspectors to H1, proxy, literature grade, and candidate rank.
- Preserve unmatched and ambiguous records as unknown.
- Separate service disruption, operational discrepancy, and confirmed physical fault.
- Use a time-forward asset-disjoint evaluation only after enough independently adjudicated outcomes exist.

External literature strengthens the theoretical validity of anomaly signs but never substitutes for these records.
