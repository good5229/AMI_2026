# LightGuard v0.10 Real-Background Validation

The card reports frozen H1 behavior on paired current-only counterfactuals over
anonymized real AMI backgrounds and a separate past-only shadow replay.

- It may display Injection Recovery, Benign Escalation, score uplift, candidate
  density, and canonical-candidate replay actions.
- It must not display field accuracy, actual fault recall, real-background FPR or
  specificity, municipal performance, or production readiness.
- Unmodified AMI is an unlabeled paired reference, not normal truth.
- The AMI has no municipal asset, rated-load, KMA, KASI, maintenance, or repair join.
- Energy remains unchanged while controlled current-only grafts are evaluated.
