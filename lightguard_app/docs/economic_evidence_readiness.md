# Economic Evidence Readiness

Access date for every source below: 2026-08-20 (Asia/Seoul).

## Decision

Cost conversion is not allowed for Suyeong-gu at this stage. In particular, the available evidence does not justify a per-dispatch cost, savings per alert, avoided truck roll, or ROI. No unrelated municipalities are combined.

## Economic evidence levels

### LEVEL A: direct, source-local calculation only

**Jeonju City remote-control announcement**

URL: https://www.jeonju.go.kr/planweb/board/view.9is?boardUid=ff8080818990c349018b1dbaa78e4b41&contentUid=ff8080818990c349018b041a87373953&dataUid=8eba577186a04f6abab704d3cdee0d17&page=794&tmpField14=

The official announcement states a two-way remote-control project covering 725 cabinets and a total project cost of KRW 1.34 billion. This is sufficient for a project-specific cabinet-deployment ratio if the claim is explicitly limited to that Jeonju project. It is not a maintenance dispatch cost, not a recurring saving, and not transferable to Suyeong.

LEVEL A is therefore admissible only for a source-local statement such as “this historical Jeonju project reported a total cost and a matching cabinet count.” It is not admissible for LightGuard's Suyeong economics.

### LEVEL B: official context, no direct LightGuard cost conversion

- **G2B / Gwangju Seo-gu, 2026 street-light underground-line unit-cost estimate**: https://www.g2b.go.kr/pn/pnp/pnpe/UntyAtchFile/downloadFile.do?bidPbancNo=R26BK01450767&bidPbancOrd=000&fileSeq=6&fileType=&prcmBsneSeCd=07. It provides official work categories and quantities for a unit-price construction schedule, including cabinet and leakage-circuit work, but not a per-dispatch denominator for LightGuard.
- **Suyeong-gu 2026 budget portal**: https://www.suyeong.go.kr/index.suyeong?menuCd=DOM_000000119001001000. It is an official route to budget documents, but the landing page alone does not identify an asset-matched contract or dispatch count.
- **Busan organization directory, Suyeong entry**: https://www.busan.go.kr/bhtelinfo02/?curPage=2254. It confirms a municipal function for street-light unit-price maintenance and materials, but contains no cost denominator.
- **Required asset portals**: Seongnam https://www.data.go.kr/data/15032441/fileData.do, Yeonsu-gu https://www.data.go.kr/data/15059623/fileData.do, Gangneung https://www.data.go.kr/data/15117413/fileData.do, and Chungju https://www.data.go.kr/data/15041822/fileData.do. They are operational asset evidence, not price evidence; they can support a future same-scope denominator only if paired with a matching official contract.

### LEVEL C: excluded from claims

The following are excluded from LightGuard economic claims: private vendor or advertisement prices; third-party tender mirrors; a municipal total budget with no asset or work denominator; a contract total divided by an invented number of dispatches; a contract from one municipality applied to another; and any blended average across Seongnam, Yeonsu-gu, Gangneung, Chungju, Gwangju Seo-gu, Jeonju, and Suyeong.

No LEVEL C material is used as evidence in the app or report.

## Required evidence for future Suyeong conversion

At minimum, a future calculation would need an official Suyeong contract or completed expenditure record with the same scope and period, a clear asset/work denominator, and a separately documented dispatch or work-order count if a per-dispatch figure is requested. The public pages reviewed here do not provide that denominator. Until it is supplied and matched, the app must use qualitative language such as “potential inspection effort reduction” and must not show a currency value.

## Anti-patterns explicitly rejected

- Dividing a municipal maintenance contract total by 204 Suyeong cabinets when the contract scope does not state that all 204 cabinets are covered.
- Dividing a project total by detector candidates and calling the result an avoided dispatch cost.
- Using Jeonju's 725-cabinet remote-control project as a Suyeong benchmark.
- Treating official procurement unit prices as actual paid costs or as recurring per-alert costs.
- Treating reduced candidate count in controlled validation as a monetary saving.
