# LightGuard v0.16 competition action utility

## Purpose

The official competition rewards business fit, feasibility, specificity,
completeness, use purpose, tangible effect, generality, and an app artifact.
v0.16 therefore separates AMI evidence into data-quality review, remote
monitoring, and a field-inspection candidate instead of treating every
non-normal H1 action as a field visit.

## Result

- Official service scope: 5/5 streetlight meters evaluable, including two
  contractually single-phase meters.
- Controlled anomaly dispatch RD: -21.95 percentage points versus collapsed
  non-normal mapping. The prospective -10 point non-inferiority target failed.
- Controlled benign dispatch RD: -2.56 percentage points. The prospective
  -10 point reduction target failed.
- The guarded policy is too restrictive for anomaly routing and does not
  reduce controlled benign dispatch enough.

## Decision

Do not tune again on the same April-June corpus. Collect a new season after
2026-06-30, freeze future meter-days before outcomes, and record data-quality,
remote-monitor, operator disposition, and field-inspection outcomes separately.

## Boundary

This is an exploratory post-hoc replay plus a B-L-12 coverage extension. It is
not independent validation, field-fault accuracy, real-background FPR or
specificity, fault probability, confirmed maintenance truth, or actual saving.
